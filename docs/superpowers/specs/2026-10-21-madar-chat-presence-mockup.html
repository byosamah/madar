Chat: who is here, Seen, typing and emoji

A mockup in the Madar chat style. The real build uses the Madar font and both themes. Yellow tags mark my guesses: correct them in the terminal.

1. A direct chat on a computer

Dot on each face in the chat list and the chat head. Head line says Online, Away or Offline. typing... replaces the last message in the list row, and shows as a line above the box in the open chat. Seen sits under your last message only, after the other person read it. The smiley in the box opens the emoji list.

3. A group or project chat: Seen by 3

Under your last message only: Seen by 3. A tap opens the names. A name counts only after that person had the chat open and in view (the rule that Madar already uses for unread). With 2 people or more typing: Reem and Faisal are typing...; with 3 or more: Several people are typing...

4. The emoji list (computer)

Opens above the smiley. Search finds English and Arabic names ("thanks", "شكرا"). Recent comes first, so the usual 2 or 3 emoji take one click. A click puts the emoji in the box where the cursor is; the list stays open for a second one. Esc or a click outside closes it. Skin tone: one choice, kept for next time.

6. The Away switch in the person menu

Madar sets the status by itself. The switch lets a person show Away by hand, for a meeting or a site visit. My guess it stays on until the person turns it off, like Slack. With it on, typing and Seen still work, and the own face shows the Away mark, so the person sees that it is on.
